01 · the hard core way

Do it the hard (core) way.

No AI tools

Nothing at home, nothing at work. Your hands, your head.

No AI search summary

Turn the overview off and read the actual sources.

No chat bots

No agents

No code review bots, no Claude, OpenCode, Pi or any model.

02 · Onarheim's Law

Agents can only maintain or increase entropy in a system. Humans are uniquely capable of decreasing it.

03 · why

Stretch your brain. Don't be a meat proxy.

  1. Keep the skill. Useful or not, know how to do it yourself.
  2. Read real PRs. Do you like reading LLM PR descriptions?
  3. Mental health. Step away from the slot machine.
  4. Perspective. See what was good and bad about the tools.
  5. Learn again. Learning takes effort and friction. That is the point.
  6. Security. Agents make wild choices and install malware by accident.
