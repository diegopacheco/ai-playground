A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

Find your flow.

Your skater rolls automatically. Press Space or ↑ to ollie over cones, benches, and planters.

While airborne, press J for a kickflip, K for a heelflip, or L for a 180. Mix different tricks in one jump to earn a combo bonus.

Land cleanly to bank points. Hitting an obstacle loses the airborne combo and 100 points. A run lasts 60 seconds.

Press P to pause. Touch controls work on phones. Changing a rider or spot starts a fresh setup. Your personal best saves on this device.
