Rational Unified Process: four phases, each one iterative, driven by use cases and architecture

entry inception · 4 stages · 6 agents · 4 loops

  1. 1inception-iteration inception → inception · until stakeholders agree on scope and cost · max 2
  2. 2elaboration-iteration elaboration → elaboration · until the architecture is stable and the main risks are retired · max 3
  3. 3construction-iteration construction → construction · until every use case is implemented and tested · max 6
  4. 4transition-iteration transition → transition · until users accept the release · max 2
